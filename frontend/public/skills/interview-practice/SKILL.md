# Interview Practice & Assessment Skill

This skill enables agents to conduct mock interviews, evaluate resume matches, and test domain-specific questions on Interviewer (`interview.nevatal.id`).

## Capabilities

- Resume-to-job matching and gap analysis
- Technical, behavioral, and situational question generation
- Answer evaluation against standard rubrics and scoring metrics
- Structured feedback reporting and interview preparation guidance
