# My Mock Interview — AI Interview Practice from Your Resume

My Mock Interview is an intelligent preparation and simulation platform that generates realistic technical and behavioral interviews tailored to your exact resume and target job description.

## Core Features

- **Resume & Job Description Analysis**: Upload your PDF/text resume and target role description to generate highly specific questions addressing your actual experience and skill profile.
- **30 Structured Interview Courses**: Comprehensive tracks covering system design, coding interviews, behavioral frameworks (STAR method), HR screening, and managerial communication.
- **180+ Practice Questions**: Realistic questions categorized by seniority, domain (Software Engineering, Data Science, Product Management, DevOps), and difficulty.
- **AI-Powered Answer Scoring**: Detailed real-time feedback evaluating technical accuracy, communication clarity, structure, and relevance.
- **Comprehensive Evaluation Reports**: End-of-interview report cards detailing strengths, growth opportunities, model responses, and practical tips.

## How to Prepare

1. **Explore Courses & Question Banks**: Review curated lessons and practice answering common technical and behavioral prompts.
2. **Configure Your Interview**: Upload your resume and paste the target job description or requirements.
3. **Conduct Mock Interview**: Answer AI-proctored questions under realistic interview pacing.
4. **Review Report Card**: Get actionable scoring breakdowns and suggested improvements for each answer.
